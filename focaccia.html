Focaccia

  • Bread
  • Italian
  • Baking
24 hrs including cold proofServes 8

A tall, airy Italian flatbread with a crisp olive-oil crust and a dimpled, salty top.

Ingredients

  • 500g bread flour
  • 400ml lukewarm water
  • 7g instant yeast
  • 10g salt
  • 60ml olive oil, plus extra for the tin
  • A few sprigs of fresh rosemary
  • Flaky sea salt, for topping

Method

  1. Whisk the flour, yeast and salt in a large bowl, then add the water and stir until no dry flour remains; the dough will be wet and sticky, which is fine.
  2. Cover and rest for 30 minutes. With wet hands, lift one side of the dough and fold it over itself. Turn the bowl and repeat on all four sides. Repeat three more times, 30 minutes apart.
  3. Drizzle with a little olive oil, cover tightly and leave to rise in the fridge overnight or up to 24 hours. The slow cold rise develops flavour and lots of bubbles.
  4. Pour half the olive oil into the baking tin, tip in the dough and leave it to warm up and spread for 2 to 3 hours, until it is puffy and jiggly.
  5. Preheat the oven to 230°C/450°F. Drizzle with the rest of the oil and press your fingertips down to dimple the dough, then scatter with rosemary and flaky salt.
  6. Bake for 20 - 25 minutes, or until a deep golden colour. Remove and rest on a rack immediately so that the crispness of the bottom remains.

Chef's Notes

Liberal with the oil in the tin as it fries the base and gives the crisp crust; if the dough springs back when you dimple it, leave it to rest for another 30 minutes.
