About

Qitao ShiMelbourne

Hey, I'm Qitao, a final-year computer science student at the University of Melbourne.

I love the creativity of cooking and building software. With both, I get to make whatever I want. They feel pretty similar to me.

Software

I've built a model that predicts ATP tennis matches from player stats, surface and recent form, and a ray tracer in C# that renders 3D scenes from scratch.

Side Quests is a Flutter app I built with a team of six at VicHack. It won three awards there. With Skanda, I co-built Wayform, a macOS app and CLI that gives coding agents a shared memory of a team's decisions.

Cooking

I started cooking at a young age. Instead of cartoons, I would regularly watch Food Network and MasterChef. Later, I worked at Breadtop, where I learnt to bake bread and make pastries.

I remember being obsessed with croissants, but I couldn't get them right in Darwin because the butter melted almost instantly. Once I moved to Melbourne, I finally nailed them. You can try my croissant recipe.

You'll also find salt bread, focaccia and dumplings on my food page. I share what I cook on Instagram as @quokachi_cook.
