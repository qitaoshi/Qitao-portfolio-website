Pork & Cabbage Dumplings

  • Chinese
  • Dumplings
  • Family style
1.5 hrsMakes about 40

Juicy pork and wombok dumplings, pleated by hand and pan-fried until crisp on the bottom.

Ingredients

  • 40 round dumpling wrappers
  • 500g pork mince
  • 1/4 wombok (Chinese cabbage), finely chopped
  • 3 spring onions, finely sliced
  • 1 thumb of ginger, grated
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Toss the chopped wombok with the salt and leave to stand for 15 minutes, then squeeze out as much water as you can using your hands, to prevent the filling from getting soggy.
  2. Combine the pork mince with soy sauce, Shaoxing wine, sesame oil and ginger, and stir hard in one direction for a couple of minutes until the mixture becomes sticky and paste-like.
  3. Fold in the squeezed wombok and the spring onions till they are evenly mixed.
  4. Hold a wrapper in your palm and add a heaped teaspoon of filling, wet the edge with water then fold in half and pleat one side against the other to seal. Leave finished dumplings under a damp tea towel.
  5. In a non-stick pan, heat a small amount of oil and lay the dumplings in flat-side down. Fry for 2 minutes until golden on the bottom side.
  6. Pour about 80ml of water into it and cover immediately. Steam for 6 minutes, then uncover and cook until the water is absorbed and the bottoms are crisp again.

Chef's Notes

Freeze extras on a tray before bagging them so they do not stick. They will cook straight from frozen taking an extra 2 minutes steaming. Serve with soy sauce, black vinegar and chilli oil.
