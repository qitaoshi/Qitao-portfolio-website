Seared Pork with Orange Sauce

  • Pork
  • Dinner
  • Sauce
40 minsServes 2

Pork fillet seared golden, sliced and served in a glossy sweet-and-sour orange sauce with sharp red onion.

Ingredients

  • 1 pork fillet, about 400g
  • 2 oranges, juiced, plus a few slices
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 20g butter
  • 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 spring onion, sliced, to serve
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Method

  1. Take the pork out of the fridge 20 minutes before cooking and pat it dry. Season all over with salt and pepper.
  2. Heat the oil in a heavy pan over medium-high heat. Sear the pork on every side until deep golden, in about 8 minutes.
  3. Reduce the heat to medium and continue cooking, turning now and then, until the centre is 63°C, about 8 to 10 minutes. Rest it on a board for 5 minutes.
  4. In the same pan, cook the garlic for 30 seconds. Add the orange juice, honey and soy sauce. Cook, scraping up the brown bits, until it thickens and coats a spoon.
  5. Put the pan off the heat and add the butter swirling it in until the sauce is glossy.
  6. Slice the pork and spoon the sauce on to the plate, lay the pork slices on top and arrange with orange slices, red onion and spring onions.

Chef's Notes

Do not move the pork as it sears, it will release from the pan when the crust has formed. The resting juices on the board go into the sauce.
